Memory History Clamp

Clamps the chat-history length the official `_memory` plugin sends to the utility model during memorize / solve. Prevents ContextOverflow errors the cascade can't recover from. Budget falls back to `_model_fallback.memory_memorize_max_chars` (50000) when the override is empty.

Advanced

Recall Method Patch

Restores the missing search_memories() method on RecallMemories in the _memory plugin v1.2.0. Without it, every recall silently fails with AttributeError. Recommended: ON.
